How to play

  • You walk the same street over and over. Memorize it.
  • See a glitch? Turn around and walk back out the way you came.
  • Nothing off? Keep going out the far door.
  • Right call: the loop counter goes up. Wrong call: it resets to 0.
  • Fill each sector's counter to break through. Six sectors to escape.
  • Every glitch you use gets patched by the Agents, so it never comes back. You'll have to spot new ones.
  • Agents aren't glitches. They're the patch. Jump over them: tap jump again in midair for a double or triple jump. From sector 3 they shoot too, so hold slow-mo and hop the bullets. On the pill screen you can make Agents Easy or Chill, or turn their shooting off. Lose all your integrity and the sector resets.
  • Blue pill (Loop mode): make it through six sectors and answer the ringing phone to escape.
  • Red pill (Trace mode): right calls raise the trace on your real body (glitches raise it most), wrong calls and hits lower it. At 100% a hardline phone rings in the loop: walk into it within 45 seconds to unplug. Watch for Hunters: when their red beam flashes, get out from under it. Lose all integrity and the signal's lost.
  • Code Sight (red pill): press C or CODE to see the code for 3 seconds and spot where the anomaly is. You start with 2 charges and earn one per glitch used.

Game notes

Déjà Glitch is a game by Buggy Games YT, the channel all about video game bugs and glitches. This time the glitches are the point.

It's inspired by two favorites: The Matrix, for the idea of a simulated world with green falling code, déjà vu that means something changed, the pill choice, a trace program that finds your real body to unplug you, seeing the world as code, hunter machines, slow-mo bullet dodging, a ringing phone as the way out, and Agents that patch the holes; and The Exit 8, the game and the movie, for the loop of walking the same hallway and spotting what's off before you're allowed to leave.

All characters, art, names, and sounds here are original. Déjà Glitch isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of The Matrix or The Exit 8.
