How to play

  • Find the 3 fuses and bring them to the fuse box by the gate.
  • Your flashlight is as bright as your real phone battery. Low battery = dim, flickering light.
  • Something lives in the dark. A strong beam pushes it back. A weak one won't.
  • Plug your phone in and the light steadies... but it can hear the charger.
  • Zombies shamble through the depot. They're slow, but they're drawn to your light. Light won't scare them, so hide or outrun them.
  • Hide (cover the light) and stay still to make things lose you.
  • Green plugs are checkpoints. With the in-game battery, stand on one to charge.
  • Keyboard: WASD/arrows move, mouse aims, Space hide, F light on/off, P pause, M mute.
  • Touch: drag the left side to move, drag the right side to aim, HIDE and LIGHT buttons.
